## One contradiction

GTMnow (Datarails) gives the VP of Brand zero revenue KPIs and says you would never approve a meme if you had to attribute pipeline to it. Growth Unhinged says brands expect quantifiable return at renewal or they will not write another cheque. Both are right at different gates: day to day latitude is bought with proof earned elsewhere (Datarails hit pipeline through paid media first), and the funder's gate still needs evidence.

## Three candidate angles

1. **Write the renewal memo before the first deal is signed.** Payoff: a founder rewrites the first 30 days of a pilot, creator deal or agency retainer so renewal evidence exists by default.
2. **The pain lane is open because nobody counts a number with a line in it** (Cannonball). Payoff: a founder finds one public, countable, deadly number in their market. Strong, but a single newsletter and the data is trucking specific.
3. **Agents are now your reader and your buyer** (Exa, Cannonball, Growth Unhinged). Payoff: publish for machine retrieval. Crowded topic everywhere already.

## Chosen angle

Angle 1. It rests on four newsletters, it contains the contradiction (soft measurement day to day, hard evidence at the gate), and it ends in an action with a date on it.

The new claim is narrower: the memo is written in the champion's voice, addressed to the budget holder who has never met you, drafted before signing with the numbers blank, and tied to a slice and a reporting date. No source says this; each describes one piece.
- *"Everywhere already."* Mutual action plans are everywhere; a blank renewal memo as a day one deliverable is not in any of the four sources.
- *"Would a founder change a decision?"* Yes: shrink the next pilot to a slice with a written baseline, and put the first two reporting dates in the calendar before kickoff.
- *"Evidence is thin."* True, and flagged: all figures are reported second hand. The article presents a habit consistent with four cases, not proof.
- Verdict: survives. It must concede early that success plans exist and must say where it breaks (slow value, gaming, second hand evidence).
