Four lines of a renewal memo

Draft them before the first deal is signed.
Step01

Claim

What will the champion say out loud?
A sentence with a number in it, not a mood.
Step02

Baseline

What is the figure today?
Agreed in writing before anything changes.
Step03

Slice

Where can the claim fail cheaply?
One region, segment, placement or creator.
Step04

Schedule

When do you report unprompted?
Early, then again. Even when the number is dull.
Leave the numbers blank. Fill them in as you report.
