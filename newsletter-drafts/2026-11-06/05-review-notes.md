## Voice check (suspect generic sentences and rewrites)
The first four rewrites below were applied to the final draft; the originals are shown for reference.
- "None of this..." replaced already; check "Meta is not the point of these figures. The point is that..." is close to the banned antithesis form. Rewrite: "These figures show the best channel changing inside twelve months, which a plan with a channel hard coded into a team cannot absorb."
- "Put the three together and the picture is plain." Generic. Rewrite: "A lead form, a model's answer and a stranger's post are now where the buyer first meets you."
- "I find that convergence more useful than any single tactic." Soft. Rewrite: "Three writers with different products to sell reached the same unit of planning, which is a list."
- "Capacity can be rented, but the definition ... cannot be." Mild antithesis. Rewrite: "Capacity is for hire. The definition of who you are trying to reach has to be written by someone on payroll."
- Proverb flip used once ("Whoever said you can't have too many channels..."). The saying is invented in form; replace with a known one if Remmy prefers ("Whoever said variety is the spice of life...").
- Elevated words used: relentless, candid. Others (hone, touchstone) could be added if Remmy wants more.
