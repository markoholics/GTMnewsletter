Four decisions behind every growth loop

1

Currency

What are you handing over?
Cash, access, status, freedom from a target.
2

Trigger

What event releases it?
Prefer one you control or can verify, close to a closed deal.
3

Clock

How often is it paid?
Match your sales cycle, not the playbook you borrowed.
4

Ceiling

What stops it?
A cap protects budget. No cap says: more of what works.
Ask the questions in order. The trigger is the one to argue over.
