# Instructions for this repository

- Weekly newsletter drafts live in `newsletter-drafts/YYYY-MM-DD/`. Nothing is ever published to LinkedIn.
- **Design:** from 23 October 2026 every issue uses the black, white and grey design in `DESIGN.md`, built with `tools/newsletter-design/build.py`. This replaces the navy and off-white brand colours mentioned in older instructions, and the newsletter is called PREVIOUSLY IN GTM. The reference issue is `newsletter-drafts/2026-10-23/`.
- Voice, sourcing and review-note rules are unchanged from the original brief.
- **Substack:** after the draft email is sent, save the issue to Substack as a draft with `tools/substack-draft/draft.py` (see its README). Drafts only. Never publish, schedule or email from Substack. If login fails, say so in the run summary and tell Mohammad to refresh `SUBSTACK_COOKIES`.
